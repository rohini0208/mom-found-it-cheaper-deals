# Mom Found It Cheaper: group launch kit

Built from current Facebook-group best practice (sources at the bottom). Everything here is a draft for you to edit.
Nothing is posted anywhere. Our own group only. No promoting in other groups until their rules allow it.

## 1. Name, tagline, privacy
- **Name:** Mom Found It Cheaper: Amazon Deals & Price Drops
  (keywords "Amazon deals" make it searchable)
- **Tagline (cover + About):** Real Amazon deals, checked daily. Prices, ratings and links in one place.
- **Privacy:** Private + Visible. Easy to find, keeps spam out, members feel it is a club.
- **Admin rule for the first 100 members:** you personally welcome every person (see section 6).

## 2. About / description (paste into the group)
> Mom Found It Cheaper is a free group for moms (and dads, and anyone who likes a good deal) who want
> Amazon price drops without the junk. Every day we share a few deals we actually checked: real photo,
> today's price, the "was" price, star rating and number of reviews. Links are in the first comment.
>
> Found a deal? Share it. Got a question before you buy? Ask. Saved money? Brag!
>
> Heads up: some links are affiliate links. As an Amazon Associate we earn from qualifying purchases,
> at no extra cost to you. Prices change, so always check the price on Amazon before you buy.

## 3. Rules (pin as a post; few, simple, with the reason)
1. **Be kind.** We are all here to save money, not to argue. Disagree politely or scroll on.
2. **Deals must be real.** Share the product and the current price. No fake or expired deals, no bait links.
3. **No spam or self-promotion.** No selling, no referral links, no "DM me" posts. It keeps the feed useful.
4. **No personal info.** Do not post addresses, phone numbers or order numbers.
5. **Check prices before you buy.** Amazon prices move. We show when we checked.
6. **Disclosure.** If you share a link you earn from, say so. We do on ours.
Breaking a rule = a friendly warning first. Repeat = removal.

## 4. Membership questions (max 3, reject blank answers)
1. What do you most like to save money on? (kids, kitchen, tech, beauty, everything)
2. Do you agree to the group rules (no spam, be kind)? Type YES.
3. Anything you wish a deals group did better?

## 5. Welcome + pinned posts
**Welcome post (pin, tag new members each week):**
> Welcome to Mom Found It Cheaper! 💛 Say hi in the comments: what is one thing you bought lately that you
> are glad you got a deal on? Every day we post a few checked Amazon deals. Turn on notifications
> (Notifications > Highlights) so you do not miss the good ones.

**Pinned "How this works" post:**
> 1) Daily deal posts, with real photo, price, was-price and stars. 2) Links are in the first comment.
> 3) Tell us what you want, and we will look for it. 4) Share your own finds with the tag "Found it!"

## 6. First 100 members: do these by hand
- Send **10 personal invites** a day to people who would honestly like it, each with a reason
  ("Thought of you because you always find the best sales"). No mass blasts.
- Welcome every new member in the comments within a day. Reply to every post in their first 30 days.
- DM each new member one line that references their answer ("Saw you like kitchen deals, today's post has a good one").
- Under 200 members the admin starts every conversation. Expect to post 3 to 5 times a week yourself.
- Do not buy members or run broad ads. They join and never engage.
- Check Group Insights weekly: active members, best posts, best posting time. Keep active members above 30%.

## 7. Weekly rhythm (same days, same times; ours: 3 PM and 5 PM ET)
| Day | Ritual | Who posts |
|---|---|---|
| Mon | **Mom's Pick**: one product, big photo, handwritten note | bot (post_single) |
| Tue | **Price Check Tuesday**: members drop a link or name, we look it up | members + admin |
| Wed | **Collage of the day** | bot |
| Thu | **Thrifty Win Thursday**: "What did you save on this week?" | members |
| Fri | **Poll**: "Which would you grab?" with 3 products from today | admin |
| Sat | **Collage of the day** + member spotlight | bot + admin |
| Sun | **Wishlist Sunday**: "What are you hunting for this week?" (we search for it) | members |

Reply to every comment in the first hour: it tells Facebook the post is alive.

## 8. Engagement prompts (rotate; open questions beat yes/no)
- What is the best $10 you ever spent on Amazon?
- Kitchen gadget you cannot live without?
- One thing you would never pay full price for?
- Show your best find under $20 (photo please).
- Which deal today surprised you most?
- Gift ideas under $25 for a teacher / sister / in-laws?

## 9. Moderation and safety
- Turn on post approval for the first 2 weeks, plus Admin Assist to decline posts with links to other stores.
- Remove spam fast, answer problems privately.
- Keep the Associate disclosure on every deal post.

## 10. Measure
Weekly, in Group Insights: new members, % active, top 3 posts, best hour. Keep what works, drop what does not.
