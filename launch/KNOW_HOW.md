# Deal-group know-how (studied Nov 16, 2026; read-only)
Opened: Smart Savers Unite, Amazon Deals Official, TFG Freebies. Directory-scanned ~40 more (size / posts per day only).

## Landscape (public groups, members / posts per day)
- TFG Freebies 248K / 90+ | Smart Savers Unite 206K / ~19-67 | Amazon Deals Official 177K / 80+
- Loads of Codes 121K / 90+ | Frugal Deal Finder 60K / 20+ | CouponThang 65K / 50+ | Tish's Freebies 42K / 60+
- Deals Finders 40K / 90+ | I Pay With Coupons 17K / 90+ | small Amazon Finds groups 3-8K / 4-90+
- Pattern: the big ones post 20-90 times a day. Admin-run, not member-run.

## What works (seen)
1. Hook headline: "No joke, You can save HUNDREDS on a TV at Best Buy! Prices From $69.99!" (TFG, text card, 22 likes/7 comments/4 shares)
2. "39% Off!" as the first words, then "Product: benefit sentence", then link (Amazon Deals Official)
3. Collage + round price badge + "Link to shop in comments" (Smart Savers)
4. Disclosure in About and in post: "(AD)(CommissionEarned)", "As an Amazon Associate I earn from qualifying purchases."
5. Freebies, giveaways, glitches and codes are in the group names: free/cheap beats "deal".
6. In-store finds with real photos (Walmart, Costco, Target, Home Depot clearance) get comments.
7. Mix: fashion, beauty, kids, home; cheap price points.
8. Rules to keep spam out: no promotions, not a buy-sell group.

## Gaps vs us
- Volume: 3-9 posts/day vs 20-90. Need smaller single-product posts too.
- No freebies/giveaway/glitch/code posts.
- Only Amazon; they cover Walmart/Target/Best Buy/Costco.
- No engagement posts (poll, "what are you buying", weekly thread).
- No in-store finds with real photos.
- Few fashion/beauty/kids items.

## Our edge (keep)
Verified was-price, ratings, price-tier "Under $10/$15/$25" posts, Mom's pick note, trust voice, tagged links, website with photos.

## To-do to make ours the best list
1. Hook headlines: "From $X", "Save HUNDREDS" only when true.
2. Single-product quick posts, 10-15/day, "NN% OFF!" + one benefit line.
3. Weekly rituals: Free Friday, Under-$10 Tuesday, Mom's pick, "what are you buying" poll.
4. Add other retailers' deals as non-affiliate "heads-up" posts (or join their affiliate programs).
5. Freebies/codes post type (only verified).
6. Real-photo posts of finds when you shop.
7. Disclosure in group About.
8. Post in other groups only if their rules allow (Smart Savers: no).

## 3. What drives comments (evidence summary)
- [O] Puzzle/quiz/fill-in cards beat deal posts by 10x to 400x on comments: 457, 339, 141, 85, 25 comments vs 1-14 on deal posts in the same groups. These posts are often AI-looking images and have nothing to do with deals.
- [O] Question posts tied to the topic ("Would you add this to your cart for $54.99?", "BEST thing you've discovered at Costco recently?") got a few answers each, far fewer than puzzles but on topic.
- [O] Deal posts with price-on-image, "ENDS TODAY" and the link in comments got 10-14 comments in the best deal-only group (Mommy & Me 2.0). Most comments were "Thank you".
- [O] Plain link posts, raw titles and code dumps got 0-3 reactions everywhere.
- [I] Meta states it demotes "engagement bait" (e.g. "check 1st comment", "comment to see"), so pure bait may hurt reach over time. I did not verify the current policy tonight.
