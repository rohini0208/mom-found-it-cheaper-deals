## What the winners share

1. **Motion in frame 1.** Every winner opens on a moving hand / person using the product, never a catalogue photo. We have no footage, so the closest substitute is a strong Ken Burns on a real in-use gallery photo (a person holding/wearing it), not the white-background hero shot.
2. **Headline text on frame 1, top-centre, 2 lines max**, high-contrast block (yellow/red or white script). Caption/subtitle lives in a dark box at the bottom, inside safe zones.
3. **Length band 16-32 s for single-creator Shorts**; list/compilation videos run to 3 min but are a different format.
4. **One product every 5-7 s** in demo Shorts; a 24 s Short carries 3-4 products or one product with 3-4 angles.
5. **Title formulas:** `<N> Amazon <category> finds that are actually worth it`, `Amazon <category> finds under $<X>`, `send this to a mom who…`, `<thing> that had NO business being this useful`. Numbers, "actually", "under $X", an audience word (mom, guy), one emoji.
6. **Shop tag / link cue** visible from frame 1 (YouTube Shop tag bottom-left). Our equivalent: the price chip early, "link in comments" late.
7. **Price is not the hook.** No winner opens on a price; price (if shown) comes after the product is understood. Opening on "$29.99" loses to opening on what it does.
8. **No stock-slideshow look.** Static photo + voice reads as an ad; winners feel like a friend filming.

## Scoring rubric for our cut (0-10 each, mean >= 9 to post)

- R1 Frame 1 shows the product in use (not a white-background hero) with visible motion.
- R2 Headline on frame 1: top-centre, <= 2 lines, high-contrast block, inside top 15 % safe zone? (must be below 15 % and above 75 %).
- R3 Length 16-32 s.
- R4 Scene cadence: no scene longer than 7 s; >= 4 visual changes.
- R5 Hook line is a benefit/curiosity statement, not a price or "deal alert".
- R6 Price reveal comes after the product is shown, with list price and % off visible together.
- R7 Title/caption follows a winner formula (number or "under $X" or audience word + "actually").
- R8 Voice pace: 2.5-3.2 words/s, no dead air > 0.6 s.
- R9 Disclosure visible but small (#AD pill), not competing with the headline.
- R10 Technical: no shimmer/jitter, steady zoom only, peak < -1.5 dBFS, captions outside top 15 % / bottom 25 %.
