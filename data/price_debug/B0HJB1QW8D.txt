About this item
DETAILS: 2026 President Donald J. Trump Semiquincentennial Dollar Coin, featuring the 1776-2026 dual date. Manganese-Brass composition
CONDITION: Uncirculated Mint State Condition
IMAGES: The images used for this listing are stock photos from our inventory. Your purchase is guaranteed to match the quality of the product shown.
COLLECTOR'S NOTE: Struck to honor 250 years of American heritage, the obverse features a portrait of the sitting President, Donald J. Trump, with the inscriptions 'LIBERTY,' 'IN GOD WE TRUST,' and '1776 - 2026.' The reverse design features the Presidential Seal, which shows an eagle holding an olive branch and a bundle of arrows, with a shield on its breast. The shield contains the additional inscription '250' to honor the Semiquincentennial of the United States.
